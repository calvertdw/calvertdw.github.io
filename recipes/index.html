General nutrition information, not medical advice. These are ordinary recipes assembled around a plant-forward, higher-protein pattern, with notes on which common reflux triggers to introduce cautiously. Anything about protein targets, supplements, or cardiovascular risk belongs in a conversation with your own clinician.

Super Veggie and the stuffed sweet potato are adapted from Bryan Johnson’s published Blueprint menu; the protein distribution follows Peter Attia’s spread-it-across-the-day approach. Neither is reproduced verbatim — portions, method, and the reflux adjustments differ deliberately.
